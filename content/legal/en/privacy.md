Effective from {{effectiveDate}}

## In short

- Posto works without an account or signing in.
- Your fasts, weight and water stay on your phone. We do not send them to any server and we have no access to them.
- We only collect anonymous visit statistics, without cookies.
- We do not sell data and we do not show ads.

## 1. Data controller

The controller of personal data is {{companyName}}, operating under the DeveloArt brand, {{address}}, tax ID (NIP) {{nip}}. For privacy matters, write to us at: {{email}}.

## 2. Data that stays on your device

Everything you enter in Posto is stored only in your browser’s storage on your device. This includes:

- the start and end times of your fasts and the protocol you chose,
- your weight measurements and water intake,
- settings such as language, theme and notifications.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no access to it, so we cannot read it, recover it or move it to another device.

You can delete it at any time in Settings (Delete all data), by clearing the site data in your browser or by uninstalling the app. Deletion cannot be undone.

## 3. Visit statistics

To know how to develop Posto, we use {{analyticsTool}}. It works without cookies and only shows us aggregate statistics, such as the number of visits, screens visited, referral source, country, device type and browser. It does not allow us to identify a specific person.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest, namely analysing and developing the app (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep statistical data for {{retentionPeriod}}.

## 4. Hosting and server logs

Posto is served from the servers of {{hostingProvider}}. Each time you open the app, the server automatically records technical data: IP address, date and time, page address, and information about your browser and operating system.

This data is needed to deliver the app and protect it against abuse (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). It is kept for {{retentionPeriod}}, in accordance with the hosting provider’s rules.

## 5. Notifications

You turn notifications on yourself in Settings, after granting permission in your browser. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone’s settings.

## 6. Contacting us

If you write to us, we process your e-mail address and the content of your message in order to reply (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep them for as long as needed to handle the matter and then for the limitation period for any claims.

## 7. Support via Buy Me a Coffee

If you decide to support Posto, the payment is handled by Buy Me a Coffee as a separate data controller, under its own privacy policy. We may receive from it the information you provide there yourself, e.g. your name or nickname and message.

## 8. Transfers outside the EEA

Our providers (hosting, statistics) may process technical data outside the European Economic Area, e.g. in the USA. This is based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.

## 9. Cookies and browser storage

Posto does not use cookies. It uses browser storage (e.g. IndexedDB) only to save your data on your device. Without it, the app could not work.

## 10. Your rights

With regard to the data we process, you have the right to:

- access your data and receive a copy of it,
- rectify and erase your data,
- restrict processing,
- object to processing based on legitimate interest,
- lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland).

Statistics are anonymous, so we usually cannot link them to you. We cannot see the data stored on your device. You manage it yourself.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change the way Posto works or the law changes, we will update this policy and let you know in the app. The current version is effective from {{effectiveDate}}.
